Toi et ton cheval, organisés.

Horsetrack réunit tout le suivi de ton cheval au même endroit : séances, soins, concours, papiers et budget. Fini les ordonnances perdues, le vermifuge oublié ou le carnet resté à l'écurie.

COACH IA
• Une intelligence artificielle qui répond d'après le carnet de ton cheval : séances, soins, dépenses, poids

SÉANCE EN DIRECT

CONCOURS

### Description

You and your horse, organised.

Horsetrack keeps everything about your horse in one place: sessions, care, competitions, papers and budget. No more lost prescriptions, forgotten worming or a care book left at the yard.

HEALTH TRACKING
• Vet, farrier, osteopath, dentist, vaccines, worming: everything logged, with the next due dates
• A reminder before every appointment (one at a time on the free plan, unlimited with Premium)
• Health record exportable as a PDF, ready to send to your vet

AI COACH
• An artificial intelligence that answers from your horse's record: sessions, care, expenses, weight
• "How many sessions this month?", "When is the next worming?": ask away (30 a month with Premium)
• A session idea every day, based on their recent weeks
• The coach does not replace your vet and gives no medical advice

LIVE RIDE
• Timer, distance, estimated gaits and GPS route, even with the screen locked
• The ride is added to your schedule
• Alert my contacts: a tracking link by email, and an alert if you stop moving for 5 minutes (Premium)

SCHEDULE AND JOURNAL
• Plan your sessions and tick them off once done
• Weekly slots: "Tuesday 6 pm, lesson" comes back every week (Premium)
• Training journal with photos and the day's weather
• Weekly and monthly summaries, and a monthly PDF report to share

COMPETITIONS
• Prepare your competitions, class by class (Premium)

PAPERS AND BUDGET
• Photograph prescriptions and invoices: they are filed and found in two seconds (Premium)
• Track your expenses and, with Premium, what is paid and what is still due
• Export a year of expenses as a PDF or spreadsheet (Premium)

TOGETHER
• Share your horse with your part-loaner, coach or groom (one person per horse): they see their schedule, care and appointments (Premium)
• Everyone gets their own rights: your part-loaner logs their rides, your groom the daily care, your budget stays yours

FREE TO START
The free plan tracks 1 horse with the schedule, calendar, journal, expenses and one reminder at a time. Horsetrack Premium adds unlimited horses, unlimited reminders, the AI coach, the document vault, sharing, detailed competitions, weekly slots, ride safety alerts, the monthly report and expense export. 1-month free trial, then €3.99/month or €39.99/year. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time in Settings > Subscriptions.
